Travel Disruption Recovery Engine — Intelligent Travel Resilience
Problem Statement
Modern travel consists of interconnected bookings such as flights, trains, hotels, transfers, tours, activities, and events. A disruption in one component can create a ripple effect across the rest of the itinerary. A delayed flight may result in a missed transfer and hotel check-in, while a cancelled activity may require changes to transportation and the day's schedule.

Travelers currently have to manually identify affected bookings, check cancellation or refund policies, search for alternatives, calculate additional costs, and rearrange their remaining plans. This becomes even more difficult when disruptions occur during an ongoing trip or when multiple components are affected simultaneously.


Hackathon Challenge
Design and prototype an intelligent travel disruption recovery platform that understands a traveler's complete itinerary, identifies the impact of disruptions, and helps create alternative recovery plans.

The solution should support different disruption scenarios such as transport delays or cancellations, missed connections, hotel or activity cancellations, transfer failures, weather-related disruptions, and traveler-initiated changes.

The platform should:
Represent the trip as a connected itinerary, identifying relationships and dependencies between bookings.
Identify direct and downstream impacts when a disruption occurs.
Generate multiple feasible recovery options by considering availability, timing, cost, refunds, cancellation policies, location, and traveler preferences.
Allow travelers to compare recovery plans based on cost, time, convenience, and the amount of itinerary affected.
Update the itinerary after a recovery option is selected.
Provide proactive recommendations or warnings for connections or activities that may be at risk.

Teams may use AI, recommendation systems, optimization, graph-based approaches, or rule-based intelligence to improve disruption detection and recovery planning.

The challenge is open-ended: teams should explore how technology can transform travel disruption management from a manual, stressful process into an intelligent and personalized recovery experience
